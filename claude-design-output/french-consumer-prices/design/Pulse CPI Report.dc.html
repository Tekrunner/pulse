Method and provenance

Amount × (headline index of the target month ÷ headline index of the origin month). Index series 011814056, Base 2025 = 100, DECIMAL(12,2), all households, France, excluding tobacco. The result is a Pulse calculation; both index levels are provider-published. INSEE publishes its own equivalent-purchasing-power converter on the same series.

Method, provenance and limits

What is provider-published

Index levels, monthly and annual changes for headline, food and energy, the four contributions, and the annual basket weights. Precision is shown exactly as published: index two decimals, percentages and contributions one decimal.

What Pulse calculates

The rent annual change, (index ÷ index twelve months earlier − 1) × 100; the rent contribution, weight ÷ 10 000 × that change; the month-over-month change of each component, from its index; and the equivalent-amount calculation.

Services and manufactured products are shown from representative series: the datasets publish their contributions but not yet an index or a weight, so both are reconstructed from the contribution and a representative annual weight (4 770 and 2 370 per 10 000). Every figure and table marks them; they are replaced series-for-series when the dataset carries them.

Limits to read with

Weights are annual, so a within-year contribution moves only with prices. Rents overlap the services contribution. The three basket shares shown do not sum to 100% — the datasets publish weights for food, energy and rents only, so the remainder is not attributable here. Base-2025 rebasing means levels are not comparable with earlier-base publications.
