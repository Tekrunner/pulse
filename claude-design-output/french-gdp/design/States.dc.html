Data-driven states inside a ready figure

Selected year beyond a section's edge

The map with 2025 selected: it draws 2024, the nearest published year, with "2024 · latest published" in its value strip, a banner naming both years and a button that selects 2024 across the report. The year shown is always stated on the map itself.

Economy without hours

New Zealand or Peru selected: GDP and GDP per inhabitant draw; the gap-to-France chart lists them beneath the bars, "published hours fail the plausibility screen; the gap is not split", instead of drawing a split.

Aggregate without a working-age share

OECD total: its gap-to-France bar splits into output per hour, hours per worker and employment per inhabitant, drawn in a grey marked "not split", because the UN publishes no working-age share for aggregates.

Derived branch rows

A88 residual industries: hatched area and swatch, a "derived" tag in the list, and the derived-rows note under the figure.

Focus and targets

Focus ring 3px #ffd166, offset 4px (the shared token). Every control is at least 44px tall, except the comparison row: its chips, add menu and OECD toggle are 28px, with 24px remove buttons, the WCAG 2.2 AA minimum target. Figure columns are click targets for the year and are mirrored by the keyboard year control and its previous/next buttons; the map's departements are mirrored by the ranked buttons and the data table.
