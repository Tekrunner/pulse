# Research Reconciliation — Observable Framework versus Evidence

## Verdict

**Pass with targeted reconciliation.** The spine adopts the research's central architectural conclusion: Observable Framework is viable only as a disposable site shell around Pulse-owned data and visual seams. AD-6 and AD-7 preserve the decisive portability boundary, and the exit drill moves the same `dataClient` and visual unchanged into a minimal Vite shell.

The later architecture decision supersedes the research report's two-candidate pilot recommendation. The spine correctly specifies an Observable-only validation pilot and must not restore comparative Evidence implementation work. Evidence remains a future explicit architecture-update option, not an active v1 candidate.

## Reconciled decision statement

Observable remains the correct adopted v1 direction, but its acceptance is conditional on one Observable production-path pilot proving the portable Pulse-owned manifest, `dataClient`, and DOM/SVG visual seams. The Vite drill is an exit test, not a competing implementation. Evidence remains rejected for v1 and dormant unless a future architecture update is triggered by materially changed evidence or a failed Observable seam.
